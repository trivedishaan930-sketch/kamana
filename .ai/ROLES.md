# Relay roles

Choose roles per task from declared strengths: **Planner** defines scope and alternatives; **Builder** implements; **Reviewer** checks evidence and consistency; **Security Auditor** checks auth, storage, RLS, uploads, secrets, abuse controls, and dependencies; **UX/UI Designer** checks clarity, mobile-first behavior, and accessibility; **QA/Debugger** reproduces and verifies failures; **Scout** records sourced free-tier and policy findings; **Librarian** maintains relay records and source links.

The author of a change may never review or score that change. A change touching auth, storage, RLS, uploads, secrets, or moderation requires a Security Auditor review before it is considered complete.
