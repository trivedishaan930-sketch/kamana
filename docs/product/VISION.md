# Kamna vision

Kamna is a calm, trustworthy AI-art gallery for discovery, respectful cultural labeling, and free approved-art downloads. It is for visitors looking for visual inspiration, creators who want a transparent moderated home for AI-generated work, and moderators who need a clear review trail.

The product principles are: disclose AI origin; moderate before public visibility; protect people and cultural traditions from misleading claims; keep the mobile experience fast; and never claim a provider, legal, or safety control that has not been verified.
