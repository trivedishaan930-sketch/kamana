# Monetization plan

Reviewed 2026-11-10. Monetization remains **off**. The owner's order is owner earnings first, then creator benefits later after real traffic. These are policy and cost findings, not a revenue forecast or approval to integrate a provider. No account, card, affiliate link, product listing, or ad code was created.

## Owner earnings: options screened

**Ads — Google AdSense.** Google's approval guidance warns that a site may be rejected for insufficient or low-quality content and says sites made mostly of images may not have enough text; it also says not to place ads on auto-generated pages or pages with little original content. The current beta has no approved gallery works, so it is not ready for an application. Google's reviewed AdSense pages did not state a subscription fee or an application card requirement; that absence is not a guarantee about every country or future setup. For payout, Google requires identity and address verification and later asks the publisher to select an available payment method; tax details may apply by location. A bank/payment method is therefore needed to receive earnings, but no credit-card requirement was found in the payment instructions. Google Search guidance says generative AI is not automatically disallowed for Search, but that does not promise AdSense approval. Google publisher policies prohibit graphic sexual content and other specified material. Keep the existing non-explicit beta and the closed 18+ collection separate from any future ad review. [1] [2] [3] [4]

**Affiliate links — Amazon.in Associates as one example.** The India agreement requires an application and permits Amazon to reject an unsuitable site; it lists explicit material, illegal activity, and IP violations among unsuitable content. Its participation requirements include a restriction whose wording appears to prohibit using Amazon special links on sites designed for mobile/handheld use. Kamna is intended to be mobile-friendly, so this is a material compatibility risk; do not add Amazon links unless Amazon confirms the current India rule applies differently to Kamna in writing. No India-specific enrollment price or card requirement was confirmed in the reviewed pages, so both are recorded as **unknown**, not as zero/no-card. No explicit AI-art-site approval promise was found. Any affiliate program also needs its own site acceptance, disclosure, payout, and content-policy check before use. [5] [6]

**Print-on-demand — Printify.** Printify advertises a free start with no credit card required, and its Help Center says account setup and product design have no upfront platform fee. For the normal connected-store workflow, however, the merchant separately pays product production, shipping, and applicable taxes using a Printify balance or a linked card; the sales channel may pay out later. That is not a guaranteed zero-cash-flow/no-card fulfillment path. Printify's Pop-Up Store avoids a separate product bill to the merchant, but the current eligible-merchant country list does not include India. The Pop-Up Store also requires Stripe verification and bank details to receive payouts. Printify's IP terms make the user responsible for rights, permissions, and non-infringement; AI-generated content still carries user review and risk. This is not a route to sell creator uploads without each creator's explicit commercial license. [7] [8] [9] [10] [11] [12]

**Standalone store — Shopify.** The official pricing page currently presents a three-day trial without a credit card, followed by a paid plan; the introductory offer and displayed currency vary by locale, and a store then incurs an ongoing subscription and payment-processing costs. It therefore does not meet the owner's zero-ongoing-cost condition. Shopify's Acceptable Use Policy puts responsibility on the merchant to follow applicable law and respect third-party rights; the reviewed policy did not promise acceptance of AI-art shops. Do not start a trial or store as a substitute for a free service. [13] [14]

**Direct print/product links.** A plain outbound link itself can be placed without a Kamna subscription, but it generates no owner income unless it is an accepted affiliate link or the owner sells a product under an approved arrangement. A creator's work cannot be sent to a printer or listed for sale merely because it can be downloaded from Kamna. The current terms explicitly reserve commercial and republication rights to the creator; obtain a separate, clear license first.

## Decision and gates

None of the reviewed options is approved for immediate use. AdSense has no upfront subscription cost stated in the reviewed help, but card requirements were not explicitly confirmed and the empty/image-heavy site is a poor application candidate. Amazon India cost/card status is unknown and its mobile-site wording conflicts with the product direction. Printify account creation is free/no-card, but standard fulfilment requires payment and the no-product-charge Pop-Up Store is not available to India-based merchants on the current country list. Shopify's no-card trial becomes paid. Plain links produce no earnings on their own.

After the site has real approved content and a measured traffic baseline, revisit one reversible owner-revenue option at a time. Before any account, ads, affiliate links, or sales feature, verify the current India terms, site acceptance, payout method, privacy impact, and full per-order or recurring costs. Ask for the owner's Yes before proceeding. Do not place monetization on adult-adjacent or closed 18+ content. Do not promise income.

## Creator benefits later

After traffic and actual creator participation, design creator attribution/provenance, profile follows, featured slots, and possibly a revenue-share model. First define how consent, attribution, calculation, payout, tax, disputes, refunds, takedowns, and termination would work. No creator payment, badge, store, print listing, or revenue-share code is authorized in this phase.
