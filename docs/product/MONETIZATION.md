# Monetization plan

Monetization is off. The order is audience first, trust second, revenue third. Possible future options are clearly labeled design slots: sponsorships, carefully reviewed affiliate links, optional creator services, or paid platform features. Each requires real traffic evidence, provider policy review for AI-generated and adult-adjacent content, a cost/privacy review, and one owner Yes. No income estimate is promised.

The free beta must not quietly become paid through usage overages. Services, limits, card requirements, and content policies belong in `docs/product/SERVICES_REGISTER.md`. If any provider requires a card for the proposed feature, stop and ask the owner instead of routing around the requirement.
