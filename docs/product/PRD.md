# Kamna product requirements

## Users and stories

A visitor can browse approved general-audience AI artwork, search by title/creator/tag, open a stable artwork page, and download an approved work. A creator can create a real account, submit an AI-generated work with rights and safety confirmations, see pending/approved/rejected status, and request removal. A moderator can review context, approve/reject/remove with a reason, and see an audit trail. An owner can configure providers and launch gates from dashboards without placing credentials in the repository.

## Priority requirements

**P0 — trust and access:** approved-only public reads; server-side authorization; AI badge and stable Kamna ID; private pending submissions; prohibited-content and rights confirmations; report/takedown intake; audit records; 18+ closed.

**P1 — discovery and creator quality:** search, tags, categories, distinct Indian tradition labels, artwork pages, creator pages, accessible mobile navigation, local preview, and free approved downloads.

**P2 — growth after evidence:** collections, creator onboarding, sharing, featured works, bilingual UI, and privacy-respecting analytics. Monetization remains off until traffic, provider policy, and owner Yes.

## Acceptance evidence

A requirement is accepted only with a reproducible check and evidence in `.ai/LOG.md` or a review file. Live provider flows are currently unknown. This PRD does not authorize app-code changes before roadmap approval.

## Non-goals

Storage-provider changes, open 18+ publishing, automatic AI-detection claims, unlimited free service promises, legal-compliance claims, and ads are non-goals in this phase.
