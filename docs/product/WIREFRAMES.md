# Wireframes and design proposals

## Proposal A — Gallery Ash editorial flow

Home → sticky discovery/search → responsive masonry → artwork detail rail → creator profile. Upload and moderation use stacked forms with persistent safety context. Strength: strong editorial identity and discovery. Risk: dense navigation on small screens.

## Proposal B — Collection-first museum flow

Home → large collection cards → tradition/category page → artwork wall → detail sheet. Upload and moderation use a stepper. Strength: clear cultural taxonomy. Risk: more taps before an artwork and higher route complexity.

## Rubric decision

The two proposals must be scored by two agents other than the author using `.ai/RUBRICS.md` after the roadmap is approved. No final selection is claimed in this docs-only phase. App code and deployment are unchanged.

## Mobile and error states

Mobile navigation collapses to Explore, Search, Upload, Account, and Report. Empty states explain the filter and offer reset. Errors show a plain message, safe retry, and no secret/provider internals. Sensitive previews never rely on hover.
