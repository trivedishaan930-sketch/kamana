# UX research

## Personas and jobs

The visitor wants quick, trustworthy visual discovery. The creator wants a clear upload, status, and rights path. The moderator wants enough context to decide safely and record a reason. The admin/owner wants simple dashboards, cost visibility, and no secret handling in chat.

The current UI strengths are a Gallery Ash visual direction, explicit safety strip, category filters, local upload preview, and visible pending/setup messages. Gaps include the smaller current route set, unverified provider flows, and no evidence from real users. A heuristic review should check system status, match to real-world labels, user control, consistency, error recovery, accessibility, and prevention of unsafe disclosure.

AI research does not replace real users. Plan a private beta with 5–10 real creators. Ask: Did you understand the AI label? Could you find your tradition/category? Did upload rules make sense? Did pending/rejection status explain next steps? Could you report or request removal? What felt unsafe or confusing?
