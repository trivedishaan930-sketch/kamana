# Market research

This is directional research, not a market-size claim. Behance positions itself around creative discovery, showcasing, and hiring; ArtStation positions itself as a showcase platform for art and design; DeviantArt combines browsing, sharing, community, and creator monetization. Kamna should not copy their brands or interaction patterns. The opportunity hypothesis is narrower: a calm AI-only gallery where provenance, human moderation, and respectful tradition labeling are first-class discovery signals.

The private-beta questions are more valuable than invented audience statistics: Can visitors tell that a work is AI-generated? Can they find a named tradition without assuming it is community-created? Can creators understand why a submission is pending or rejected? Can moderators finish review with a reason and audit record? Results must be logged after 5–10 real creators, not simulated by AI.
