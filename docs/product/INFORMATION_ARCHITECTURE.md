# Information architecture

Public discovery sits at the home/gallery layer. Category and tradition pages narrow the gallery without flattening Indian traditions. An artwork page is the durable share and download surface. Creator profiles expose only approved public works and public profile fields. Upload and account are authenticated flows. Report/takedown is available from artwork context and a direct route. Moderation is a separate noindex staff area with server-side checks.

Navigation must keep the AI-generated badge, audience rating, and safety context close to artwork actions. Empty states must explain whether no approved work exists, a filter is too narrow, or provider setup is unavailable. Errors must never reveal service-role details or private records.
