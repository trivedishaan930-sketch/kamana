# Value and business model

Visitors should gain a calm way to discover clearly labeled AI art, find useful categories and search, and view or download approved images without signing in. Creators should gain a moderated showcase with clear provenance, creator credit, and honest status. The owner wants an audience and trust first, then the owner's own earnings, and only later creator benefits. No income estimate is promised.

The owner-revenue screen was reviewed on 2026-11-10. This compact cost view records the current public terms, not a guaranteed quote. Costs, offers, card rules, country support, and account limits must be rechecked before use.

| Option | Current cost/card result | Fit and gate |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdSense | No subscription fee or application-card requirement stated in the help pages reviewed. A payout method, identity/address checks and location-specific tax details may be needed later. | Do not apply while the gallery has no approved works. Google warns that mostly-image or insufficient-content sites may be rejected. Approval for an AI-art gallery is unknown. [1] [2] [3] |
| Amazon.in Associates | India-specific joining cost and card requirement remain unknown from the reviewed terms. | The India participation text appears to restrict links on mobile-designed sites; seek written clarification. No AI-art acceptance guarantee was found. [4] [5] |
| Printify standard POD | Free account/design creation and no card to start; each standard order costs production plus shipping/tax, charged to balance or card separately from storefront payout. | Not no-cash-flow/no-card at order time. Creator-owned uploads need a clear commercial license. [6] [7] [8] |
| Printify Pop-Up Store | Printify does not separately charge the seller for products in this channel; Stripe verification and bank payout details are required. | Current eligible merchant country list excludes India. Not available to the owner on the list reviewed. [9] [10] |
| Shopify store | Three-day no-card trial, then a paid plan; temporary introductory price varies by locale. Payment processing may add fees. | Not zero ongoing cost. The reviewed AUP gives no AI-art approval guarantee. [11] [12] |
| Plain outbound product link | No Kamna provider fee merely to display a link. | No owner earnings unless accepted as affiliate or the owner has a separate product/contract. No creator artwork sales without explicit license consent. |

The current hosting, database, storage and email service costs cannot be recalculated from public pages alone. Dashboard usage, billing status and current quota headroom are unknown; see `SERVICES_REGISTER.md` and `research/provider-policy-2026-11-09.md`. Do not describe the beta as guaranteed to remain free.

The sequence is to improve useful discovery, measure search/mobile/performance baselines, run a small private beta, and establish actual traffic and moderation workload. Only after the site contains useful approved work should the owner compare current monetization terms, review cost and privacy effects, and approve one reversible owner-earnings experiment. After traffic and creator participation, design creator credit, follows, featured slots, and possibly revenue sharing. Revenue sharing needs licensed rights, transparent calculations, payout/tax operations, disputes and takedown terms before any build.

AI-art copyrightability and possible infringement are jurisdiction-specific questions, not a product promise. The U.S. Copyright Office is publishing a multipart study that includes generative-AI output copyrightability; that is not legal advice for India. Ask qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction to review ownership, prompt/tool terms, source-image rights, likeness, and print licensing before selling any work. [13]

Visitor को free और आसान AI-art discovery मिलेगी।
Creator को credit और बाद में growth के अवसर मिलेंगे।
Owner पहले audience और trust बनाएगा, फिर कमाई जाँचेगा।
अभी किसी earning option का launch मंज़ूर नहीं है।

किसी creator की art बेचने से पहले उसकी साफ़ अनुमति चाहिए।

कमाई की कोई guarantee नहीं है।
