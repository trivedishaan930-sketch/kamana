# Value and business model

Visitors gain a calmer way to discover labeled AI art. Creators gain a moderated place with clear provenance and status. The owner gains an audience first, trust second, and only later a possible revenue path. Every cost or limit is an assumption until verified from the provider dashboard and current terms.

The sequence is: prove useful discovery; run a small private beta; measure retention and moderation workload; review policies and costs; ask owner Yes; then test one reversible monetization slot. No income promise, ad commitment, or paid badge is made. Storage remains separate.

## Owner summary

Visitor को trustworthy discovery मिलेगा।
Creator को clear status और provenance मिलेगा।
Owner पहले audience और trust बनाएगा।
Revenue बाद में, policy check के बाद आएगा।
Income promise नहीं है।
