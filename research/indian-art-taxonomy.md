# Indian Folk & Traditional Art Taxonomy for Kamna

Kamna should treat Indian visual traditions as distinct, regionally named practices rather than one generic “folk art” filter. A parent collection can organize discovery, while each tradition has its own description and search tags. Some forms are community-based folk traditions; others are regional painting or textile crafts; the sources do not support forcing every form into a folk/tribal/classical binary.

## Recommended subcategories

- **Madhubani / Mithila painting (Bihar):** A regional folk painting tradition associated with domestic and ritual wall/floor art, later also made on paper and cloth. Common visual references include outlined figures, dense pattern-filled space, mythology, plants and animals. Suggested tags: `Mithila painting`, `Madhubani`, `Bihar folk art`, `ritual painting`, `natural motifs`. [1] [2]
- **Warli painting (Maharashtra):** A living community-based tradition associated with the Warli community. Its familiar visual vocabulary includes fine white geometric figures against an earthen ground, showing village life, farming, ritual and dance. Keep it separate from Gond and avoid treating it as a frozen or uniform template. Suggested tags: `Warli`, `Maharashtra`, `community tradition`, `geometric figures`, `village life`. [3] [4]
- **Gond painting (Central India):** A living Indigenous/community tradition with household wall/floor practices and contemporary painting forms. Nature, animals, plants and mythic subjects may be rendered with rhythmic dots, dashes, lines and patterns. Do not reduce the tradition to one exact template. Suggested tags: `Gond art`, `Madhya Pradesh`, `nature motifs`, `dot-and-line patterning`, `natural pigments`. [5] [6]
- **Odisha Pattachitra:** A regional traditional painting practice associated with Odisha, including cloth and palm-leaf forms. Common examples use precise outlines, flat colors, decorative borders and devotional or narrative subjects. Suggested tags: `Odisha`, `Puri`, `Raghurajpur`, `traditional painting`, `narrative`, `palm-leaf art`. [7] [8]
- **Rajasthani Phad painting:** A Mewar-region painted-scroll and storytelling tradition, commonly associated with Shahpura. Its long cloth compositions arrange a narrative in episodes around important figures. Keep it distinct from Telangana’s Cheriyal scroll tradition. Suggested tags: `Rajasthan`, `Mewar`, `narrative scroll`, `painted cloth`, `folk storytelling`. [9] [10]
- **Kalamkari textile art (Andhra Pradesh):** A textile-art tradition with distinct subpractices, including Srikalahasti freehand painting and Machilipatnam/Pedana block printing. Do not collapse hand-drawn and block-printed work into one technique. Suggested tags: `Andhra Pradesh`, `painted textile`, `block printing`, `natural dyes`, `narrative and floral motifs`. [11] [12]
- **Cheriyal scroll painting (Telangana):** A regional painted-scroll tradition associated with storytelling and local narratives. Typical references include a vivid red ground, sequential story panels, bold outlines and ornamental borders. Keep it distinct from Phad. Suggested tags: `Telangana`, `narrative scroll`, `red-ground painting`, `folk storytelling`, `Nakashi tradition`. [13] [14]
- **Thanjavur / Tanjore painting (Tamil Nadu):** A South Indian regional painting tradition often organized around prominent figures, rich color, ornate details and raised gilded decoration. The reviewed sources support “traditional painting” as a safe category; they do not require calling it folk, tribal or classical. Suggested tags: `Tamil Nadu`, `South Indian painting`, `gold leaf`, `raised relief`, `wood-panel painting`. [15] [16]

## Additional general discovery categories

Alongside the named traditions, the public gallery can organize AI artwork into Festival & Celebration, Nature & Wildlife, Fantasy & Mythology, Portrait & Character, Fashion & Editorial, Architecture & Places, and Abstract & Experimental. These are discovery categories, not claims about traditional cultural provenance. The 18+ Fashion & Swimwear collection should remain separate, be age-gated and limited to fictional clearly adult subjects in non-nude, non-explicit depictions; it is not a pornographic category.

Any image inspired by a living or regional tradition should be labeled as AI-generated and “AI-inspired,” not passed off as a traditional/community-created object or as an authentic work by a named artist. Keep the tradition name and region available as provenance/search metadata, and state that the image is an AI interpretation.
