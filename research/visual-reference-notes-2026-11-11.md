## Empty states

Toptal's UX article describes an empty state as a moment with nothing to display and recommends that useful empty-state messaging explain what is happening, why it is happening, and what the user can do next. It also separates first-use, no-results, and error states, and notes that dynamic messages should remain available to screen-reader users through accessible announcements. Apply this by keeping category navigation visible while showing distinct loading skeletons, honest empty/no-results content, and retryable errors rather than using pretend artworks or provider jargon. Source: [Toptal — Empty States: The Most Overlooked Aspect of UX](https://www.toptal.com/designers/ux/empty-state-ux-design).
